Who we are

bom lab is provided by Linda Dao, Danziger Straße 77, 10405 Berlin, Germany. Contact: lindadao92@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account. Your answers, photos, reports, routine and chat history are stored on your phone only.
  • If you back up your iPhone, to iCloud or to a computer, Apple includes this app's data in that backup like any other app's. That backup is yours and is controlled by your Apple settings. We cannot see it.
  • Photos leave your phone only if you agree to AI photo analysis. You are asked before the first photo, and you can switch it off in Settings at any time.
  • Chat messages leave your phone only if you agree to AI chat. You are asked once, after the questionnaire, and you can switch it off in Settings at any time.
  • We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Questionnaire answers, including age, skin concerns, sensitivity, medication and pregnancy answers.
  • Selfies, skin reports, face asymmetry reports, color analysis results and your routine.
  • Chat history, log notes and the products you searched for.

Face asymmetry is measured on your phone. That photo is not sent anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Settings also has buttons to delete photos, face and color reports, chat history, or everything.

What leaves your phone, and when

What When Who receives it
A selfie When you take a check-in, a detailed skin analysis or a color analysis, and only with AI photo analysis switched on Our server, then Anthropic
A photo of a product pack When you scan a product, with AI photo analysis switched on Our server, then Anthropic
The product name you type When you search the web for a product Our server, then Anthropic, which also runs a web search for it
Chat messages, with your questionnaire answers, routine, recent log notes and skin analysis results When you use AI chat, and only with AI chat switched on Our server, then Anthropic
Your latest selfie, together with the chat message above When you use AI chat, and only with both AI chat and AI photo analysis switched on Our server, then Anthropic
A random install id With each request above Our server only

Your name is never sent. Questionnaire answers are not sent with photos you take for analysis or with product photos. In AI chat, your latest selfie and your answers are sent together, so the answer can be about your skin.

Our server

Our server passes requests to the AI model and returns the answer. It does not store photos, messages or answers, and does not write them to logs. It logs only the type of request, the result code and the time taken.

The random install id is created on your phone. It contains nothing about you. The server uses it to limit how many requests one phone can make, keeps the count in memory for at most a day, and does not store it.

The server is hosted by Railway (Railway Corporation, United States) in the Netherlands (Railway's EU West region).

Anthropic

The AI model is Claude, run by Anthropic, PBC (United States). Anthropic processes the data above only to produce the answer, as our processor, under its Commercial Terms of Service and the Data Processing Addendum that forms part of them.

  • Training: Anthropic does not use data sent this way to train its models.
  • Retention: Anthropic deletes what we send, and the answers, within 30 days. It may keep them longer where the law requires it, or for up to 2 years (and safety scores for up to 7 years) if its automated systems flag a request as breaking its usage policy.
  • Where: Anthropic stores data in the United States and may process requests in the United States, Europe, Asia and Australia.

For people in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, this transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, with the UK and Swiss addenda, which are part of Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase is active, not your payment details.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

  • AI photo analysis and AI chat: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.
  • Health-related answers and face photos may count as special category data. They stay on your phone unless you consent as above.
  • Request limiting with the install id: our legitimate interest in preventing misuse.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data yourself in the app, since it is stored on your phone. For anything else, or to complain, contact us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

bom lab is for people aged 13 and over. The app asks your age and stops if you are younger. If you are under 18, it recommends no active ingredients.

Not a medical service

bom lab is a wellness and education app. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
